Beyond Us 跨越

2024 | Taiwan | 11 min
Directed, Shot, Edited by August Mai

Mayo Lai, a multi-talented actor, educator, and animal rights activist from Taiwan is driven by a deep fascination with primates and a desire to challenge societal norms, she embarks on an unconventional quest to befriend the chimpanzees residing at the Taipei Zoo with the aim of one day performing for them.
